RAINWEAVER

Guide a luminous unicorn through an endless 3D forest as darkness closes in behind you.

Cast a thread of light to catch branches, build momentum through each swing, and release at the right moment to cross the next gap. Avoid moving thorns, leave fragile pink branches before they break, and collect golden dew for bonus points. The farther you travel, the harder survival becomes.

A custom WebGL2 renderer brings the forest to life with procedural geometry, dynamic shadows, layered bloom, and animated foliage. Synthesized music accompanies each flight, while a dissolving trail of light marks its end. Everything fits inside a 13KB ZIP, with no external assets or runtime libraries.

Controls:
- A / D or arrow keys: move and swing
- Space: jump
- Hold E or left mouse button: catch a branch
- Release: let go and carry your momentum
- Shift while attached: shorten the thread
- R: restart
- Esc: pause
- M: mute / unmute

Requires a keyboard and a WebGL2-capable browser.
